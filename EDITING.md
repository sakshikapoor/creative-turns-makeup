# How to update the Creative Turns website

You don't need to install anything. Everything happens on one web page.

**Your link:** `https://creativeturns.netlify.app/admin/` — bookmark it.

You'll sign in twice, once each:

1. **A grey box pops up asking for a username and password.** Sakshi will send
   you these. Tick "remember" if your browser offers to.
2. **Then click "Login with GitHub"** and sign in with your GitHub account.

After the first time your browser remembers both, and you'll go straight in.

> Seeing the grey box again? That's normal after clearing your browser history or
> switching to a different device. Just type them in again.

---

## What you'll see

Three groups down the left:

- **Products** — every product section and all the photos
- **Page wording** — the big headline, the quote, the gifting text, the contact banner
- **Site details** — business name, phone number, Instagram, and the top menu

Click any one to start editing.

---

## Changing words

Click into any box and type. That's it.

The headline box has a small trick: put `*asterisks*` around a word to make it
*slanted*. So typing

```
Crochet that turns *everyday* into something
```

puts the word "everyday" in slanted pink, exactly as it looks now.

---

## Adding or replacing a photo

1. Go to **Products** → **Product sections & photos**.
2. Open the section you want (click its name to expand it).
3. Under **Photos**, either click an existing photo to swap it, or click
   **Add photo** at the bottom for a new one.
4. Click the **Photo** box → **Upload** → choose the picture from your computer.
5. Fill in:
   - **Caption** — the text that shows over the photo, e.g. "Blossom Pink"
   - **Photo description** — a sentence describing the picture for people who
     can't see it, e.g. "Pastel pink crochet bow hair clip"
   - **Photo focus** — leave alone unless the photo gets cropped badly. If heads
     or flowers get cut off, try **top**.

**Please keep photos under about 500 KB.** Anything straight off a phone is
usually much bigger — WhatsApp a photo to yourself and save it back, or use any
"compress image" website first. Big photos make the site slow to load.

---

## Seeing your change before it goes live

This is the important part.

1. Click **Save**. Your change is now a *draft* — the live site has not changed.
2. You'll land on the **Workflow** board with your change in the **Drafts** column.
3. Wait about a minute. A **Check for Preview** button appears — click it.
   It opens a temporary copy of the site showing your change.
4. Look it over. Not happy? Go back and keep editing, then Save again.

## Making it live

1. On the Workflow board, drag your change to **Ready** (or use the status menu).
2. Click **Publish**.
3. Wait about a minute, then refresh the real site. Done.

---

## Things worth knowing

- **Nothing is ever really lost.** Every change is recorded. If something looks
  wrong, message Sakshi and it can be put back exactly as it was.
- **Don't change "Menu link name."** Those short words (`hair`, `bouquets`) are
  what the top menu uses to jump to each section. Renaming one breaks that link.
- **You can't delete a whole section.** That's on purpose. Ask Sakshi if a section
  genuinely needs to go.
- **Half-finished is fine.** Save as a draft and come back later — it stays in
  Drafts until you publish it.
- **If the preview link 404s,** the build probably hasn't finished. Wait a minute
  and click Check for Preview again.
